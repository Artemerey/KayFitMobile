=== English (≤ 4000 chars) ===

Kayfit 1.3.1

• Fixed calorie targets after onboarding: weight loss, maintenance, and muscle gain now produce the correct type of plan
• Muscle-gain goals can no longer receive an extreme 1,200 kcal deficit
• Your selected weight-loss pace is now preserved and used in the calculation
• Added safeguards against excessively aggressive calorie deficits

• Исправлен расчёт калорий после онбординга: похудение, поддержание и набор мышц теперь дают разные корректные планы
• При цели набора мышц больше не назначается экстремальный дефицит 1200 ккал

=== Previous release: 1.3.0 ===

Kayfit 1.3.0

• Voice input no longer cuts off on pauses — dictate long meals; text now accumulates and recording stops only when you tap stop, background the app, or leave the screen
• Live camera preview on the food capture screen (was a static placeholder)
• Photo recognition survives backgrounding — if you switch away while a photo is being recognised, it resumes correctly on return
• Photo no longer hangs on "recognising" after an immediate background/resume right after capture
• Chat opens at the newest message; the photo bubble no longer gets lost in the feed
